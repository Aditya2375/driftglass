Pointwise central 90% ranges, not a 90% chance of staying inside the band for the whole path. Monthly observations can miss movements between observations.

More paths reduce sampling noise within this model. They cannot fix a wrong drift, volatility, or distribution. At high volatility and long horizons, rare right-tail values can dominate the mean and make its estimated standard error unstable.

THE EQUATION

Simple by design.

S(t + Δt) = S(t) exp[(μ - σ²/2)Δt + σ√Δt Z]

Z is standard normal; Δt = 1/12 year. This exact GBM transition avoids Euler discretization error at the monthly observation times. It does not make the model an exact description of a market.

Exact mean: S₀ exp(μT). Exact median: S₀ exp[(μ - σ²/2)T]. Higher volatility lowers the median at fixed drift, but not the arithmetic mean. No risk-neutral pricing is done here.

WHAT THE MODEL LEAVES OUT

Know the glass.

Constant drift and volatility. Independent normal log increments. No jumps, volatility clustering, changing regimes, dividends, financing, inflation or trading costs. No bankruptcy: GBM stays positive.

Inputs are assumptions, not estimates. A central range is not a worst case, and a sample minimum is not a loss limit. No recommendations, allocations or trades are made.

The portable Park-Miller generator and Box-Muller transform make this teaching sample reproducible. They are not cryptographic or a production risk engine. Small last-digit differences between platforms are possible.
